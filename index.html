How the agent works

1. ObserveScans, messages, order data
2. ScoreRisk 0 to 100, confidence
3. Choose actionUpdate, exchange, refund, evidence
4. RouteAutomatic, approval or monitor
5. Learn120-day outcome, edits
Feedback loop: outcomes and merchant edits recalibrate scoring and thresholds

Merchant controls

Runs without approval

  • Status updates from pre-approved templates
  • Evidence pack preparation, kept internal

Always needs approval

  • Exchange offers
  • Refunds above the cap, or any refund while automatic refunds are off
  • Any order with confidence below 50%

Permissions and trust

Agent can read

  • Orders, card payment status and dispute records
  • Shipment status and customer messages linked to the order

Agent can do

  • Send templated messages
  • Issue refunds within the cap, after approval where required

Agent cannot do

  • See full card details or change prices
  • Message customers outside approved templates
  • Delay or discourage a refund the customer is owed

Failure handling

  • Courier data missing or stale: review only, no action
  • Daily budget reached: refunds move to approval
  • Every action is logged with the signals used
  • Sent messages cannot be recalled, so templates are approved in advance

How Razorpay earns

  • Success fee on disputes avoided, or a paid Agent Studio tier
  • Lower dispute handling time and cost for Razorpay (see the estimator)
  • Fewer settlement holds, which supports merchant retention
  • Dispute ratio as a possible input to credit offers (speculative)

Why a fixed rule is weaker

  • A rule such as refund after 7 days of delay ignores value, scan history, messages and category
  • The agent weighs these together and picks one of four actions
